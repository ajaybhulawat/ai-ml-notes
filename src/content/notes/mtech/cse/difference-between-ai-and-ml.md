## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broader concept of machines performing tasks intelligently.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables machines to learn from data.

## Key Differences

1. AI is the goal.
2. ML is the approach.
3. AI includes ML, NLP, Robotics.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | Artificial Intelligence | Machine Learning |
|---------|------------------------|------------------|
| Scope | Broad | Narrow (subset of AI) |
| Goal | Simulate human intelligence | Learn from data |
| Examples | Robotics, NLP, Expert Systems | Regression, Classification, Clustering |
| Data Dependency | May or may not need data | Always needs data |

## Summary

- AI is about creating intelligent machines.
- ML is about teaching machines using data.
- All ML is AI, but not all AI is ML.
