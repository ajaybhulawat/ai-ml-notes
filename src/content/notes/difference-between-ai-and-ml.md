## Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the broader concept of machines performing tasks intelligently.

Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables machines to learn from data.

## Key Differences

1. AI is the goal.
2. ML is the approach.
3. AI includes ML, NLP, Robotics.
